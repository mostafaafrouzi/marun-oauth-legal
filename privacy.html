Privacy Policy — Marun Ads Marketing

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Marun Ads Marketing (“the App”) is an internal OAuth client used by Marun Agency to access Google Ads APIs on behalf of authorized operators. This policy explains what data is handled when you authorize the App.

Data we access

With your consent, the App may access Google Ads account data needed for campaign management and reporting (for example account IDs, campaigns, ads, keywords, and performance metrics). The App requests the Google Ads API OAuth scope only.

How we use data

Data is used solely to operate Marun Agency marketing tooling (read/report and authorized campaign changes). We do not sell personal data. Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored securely on our private servers for continuous API access.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and operational logs are retained only as long as needed to provide the service or meet legal obligations. You may revoke access anytime in your Google Account security settings.

Sharing

We do not share Google Ads data with third parties except Google (as the API provider) or when required by law.
